I am **Zhongqi FAN**, an undergraduate student in Applied Mathematics at **Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University (BNBU)**. My research interests include theoretical ML, selective classification.

## Research

I recently completed a project on **Guided Clustering-based Uncertain Learning (GCUL)**, a geometry-driven framework for selective classification. The work derives a theoretical feasibility criterion for when cluster-guided rejection can provide positive operational utility, and validates it on real-world emotion classification benchmarks.
